# 01 – Monolith, modulith, and microservices — structure and trade-offs

We distinguish code structure, running processes, and release units, then use these views to compare monoliths, modular monoliths, and microservices. The chapter starts with internal boundaries and examines the benefits and costs introduced by network separation.


- [01.01. Architecture: code, processes, and deployment](01-01-architecture-dimensions.md) — distinguishing source organization, runtime processes, and deployment units.
- [01.02. The monolith: a simple boundary with an intricate internal structure](01-02-monolith.md) — the consequences of shared releases, local calls, and internal complexity.
- [01.03. The modulith: a modular monolith with internal contracts](01-03-modular-monolith.md) — module boundaries, data ownership, and internal interfaces.
- [01.04. Microservices and the distributed monolith trap](01-04-microservices-and-tradeoffs.md) — independence, network costs, and hidden coupling.
