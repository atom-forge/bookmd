# Assignment 4 – Defining the problem and exploring opportunities

Formulate the project's central problem based on research and the persona, then examine several possible solution directions. The goal is to choose a justified focus rather than defend the first idea.

## Deliverables

- A shareable FigJam link;
- A problem statement using this structure: “The problem for [user type] is [situation or obstacle], which causes [negative consequence].”;
- At least three “How Might We…?” questions;
- Solution ideas based on those questions;
- A 2×2 prioritization matrix with impact and feasibility axes;
- A brief explanation of which solution direction you will pursue and why.

Clearly distinguish research evidence from unverified assumptions on the board.
