# Submission guidelines

After each session, submit the relevant material for your own project: research notes, a diagram, a Figma link, or a brief decision log. Name files clearly and make sure external links can be opened without requesting access.

Do not submit personal data. Record interview participants only with their consent, and anonymize your notes. Test participants are not assessment tools: they may stop at any time and should not feel that difficulties with the interface are their own fault.
