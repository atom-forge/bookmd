# Assessment

Assessment measures design thinking, not just the visual appeal of the final screens.

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Understanding the problem, research, and insights | 25% |
| Information architecture and task flow | 20% |
| Wireframes, prototype, and accessibility | 20% |
| Testing, iteration, and decision log | 25% |
| Presentation and professional communication | 10% |

A strong submission clearly distinguishes facts, assumptions, and decisions. A finished prototype alone does not compensate for missing research or testing.
