# Assignment – Project presentation

Create a 5–10-minute project presentation using FigJam or Figma. Present an evidence-based story leading from the problem through research and decisions to the final prototype, rather than a list of screens.

## Deliverables

- A shareable presentation link or the slides;
- A link to the final Figma prototype;
- The presentation should include:
  - The project topic and why you chose it;
  - The problem, target user, and important pain points;
  - The main lessons from research, the persona, and the journey;
  - Selection of the solution direction and the main design decisions;
  - Feedback received and the changes made in response;
  - The final prototype and what you are most proud of;
- A brief self-reflection on your strongest evidence and what you would investigate or develop further.

The presentation should be understandable on its own: make clear which observation, research, or design decision supports each claim.
