# Assignment – User research and user journey

Develop a better understanding of the target group identified in the first milestone. Conduct at least one brief interview or survey; if you conduct an interview, ask about past behavior and specific experiences rather than whether participants like your idea.

## Deliverables

- A shareable FigJam link;
- An interview or survey summary: the participant's anonymized, relevant background, the questions, important answers, and lessons learned;
- At least two insights that influence the design;
- A simple user journey map for a specific task, showing steps, feelings, pain points, and opportunities for improvement.

The journey should trace a single, clearly defined task. Research notes must not contain personal or sensitive information.
