# Assignment – Clickable prototype and initial feedback

Connect your wireframes into a clickable Figma prototype in which the selected main task can be completed. Then have at least one fellow student try it: observe where they become uncertain rather than verbally guiding them through it.

## Deliverables

- A shareable Figma prototype link;
- A clear starting screen and success state;
- Working connections and any necessary transitions for the main flow;
- At least three specific pieces of feedback or observations;
- A brief reflection: what surprised you, what was useful, and what you would change.

You do not need to make every possible function interactive. The prototype supports trying the main task, so indicate its known limitations too.
