# Assignment – Exploring ideas and problems

Create a FigJam board about a digital product, service, nonprofit application, or educational software based on your own idea and connected to your hobbies or professional interests. The goal is an initial map of a clear, real problem situation, rather than a finished solution.

## Deliverables

- A shareable FigJam link;
- A board using sticky notes, arrows, or simple blocks to show the specific problem, whom it affects, when it occurs, the user's goal, and what you want the solution to achieve;
- A brief visual first pitch communicating the core idea.

Choose an observable situation and task rather than a general topic. The board does not need to be a persuasive sales pitch: make it honest, brief, and easy to follow.
