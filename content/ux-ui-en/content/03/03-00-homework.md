# Assignment – Persona, target group, and inclusion

Synthesize your research findings into a fictional, research-based persona. The persona should help explain the goals, digital habits, and obstacles users bring to the earlier user journey.

## Deliverables

- A shareable FigJam link;
- At least one persona: name, age, occupation or life situation, digital habits and technical confidence, goals, motivations, and pain points;
- An updated version of the user journey from Assignment 2, from the persona's perspective;
- Three usability or accessibility risks that the persona may encounter during the main task, each with a brief direction for prevention or improvement.

Do not build the persona from stereotypes: connect every significant claim to research observations or a clearly labeled assumption.
