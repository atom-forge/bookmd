# Tools and technical setup

- A browser and a note-taking tool;
- Figma or another collaborative prototyping tool;
- Shareable storage for research and project materials;
- The ability to share your screen or conduct in-person testing.

Tools support your thinking. Hand-drawn wireframes are also acceptable if they are legible and communicate the task flow clearly.
