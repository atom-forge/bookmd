# Presentation template

1. What situation and user did you investigate?
2. What was the evidenced problem, and what was the success criterion?
3. What did the research reveal?
4. How did you structure the task flow and prototype?
5. What did you test, what did you find, and what did you change?
6. What questions remain open, and what would the next step be?

Do not present the entire Figma file. Choose screens that demonstrate a decision in your story or a change resulting from testing.
